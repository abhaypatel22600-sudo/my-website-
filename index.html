NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT:-

NCERT DEFINITION:-

National development->
the process that overall progress and improving of a country in social,economic,education,technology field.

Average Income->
the average income is the total income of a country divide by its total population . it is known as per capita income.

Literacy Rate->
the percentage of people whose age 7 years and above able to read and write is called literacy rate.

Infant Mortality Rate->
It indicates the chidren that die before one years as proporation of one thousand children .

Net Attendence Ratio->
The proportion of children of official school age who are enrolled in school,indicating access to and the quality of education.

Purchasing power parity(ppt)->
PPP is a measure of the price of specific goods in different countries and is ised to conpare the absolute purchasing power of the countries'currencies.
